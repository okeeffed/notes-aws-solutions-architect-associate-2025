# AWS Billing Conductor (ABC) #card

AWS Billing Conductor (ABC) facilitates **showback and chargeback workflows** for AWS Solution Providers and Enterprise customers.

---

## ABC Key Features #card

- **Group Accounts** – Create mutually exclusive **billing groups** to aggregate and compute usage separately
- **Custom Pricing Plans** – Apply **global, service, billing entity, or SKU-specific** markups/discounts
- **Custom Line Items** – Add **one-time or recurring** charges/credits as **flat or percentage-based**
- **Pro Forma Cost Analysis** – View **cost projections** based on pricing configurations
- **Billing Group Cost Reports** – Generate a **Cost & Usage Report (CUR)** per billing group
- **Margin Analysis** – Compare **applied rates vs AWS actual rates** over time

---

## ABC Benefits #card

- **Customizes billing rates** per customer or business unit
- **Allocates credits & charges** as per requirements
- **Distributes overhead expenses** without affecting AWS billing
- **Provides cost visibility & forecasting** via AWS Cost Explorer

---

## ABC Pricing #card

- **First 62 days free**
- **First 500 accounts** → $8.25/account/month
- **Next 1,500 accounts** → $6.75/account/month
- **Beyond 2,000 accounts** → $5.25/account/month
